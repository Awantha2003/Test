# Rakawala: Research Notes

> **Rakawala** (රැකවල, "protection / guard") is a child-safety SaaS for parents in Sri Lanka and beyond.
> It covers live location, safe-place alerts, SOS (button + voice), and an admin-run subscription business.

## 1. The problem: what can actually happen to a child

Sri Lankan context (2024–2025):

- Officials reported **15,000+ child abuse reports in 2024**. The NCPA's own count was 8,746 cases, which measures something different.
  Both are described as "the visible part of the problem".
- The Minister of Women and Children's Affairs listed **homes, schools, detention centres and public roads** as places where children are harmed.
- **School and public transport** (school vans, buses, trains) is a recurring setting in reported incidents. A CCTV-in-buses proposal followed.
- **Child trafficking**: 49 alleged incidents with 73 victims in 2025. Colombo District had the most.
- **Missing or abducted children** show up in monthly media counts. There is no reliable official annual figure.

Everyday risks that parents raised with us, and that competitors also design around:

| # | Real-world situation | What the app must do |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Child doesn't arrive at tuition class / school on time | Scheduled "arrive-by" check → **Late alert** to parent |
| R2 | Child leaves school or class early or unexpectedly | Geofence **exit alert** |
| R3 | Child is in danger: followed, harassed, lost, injured | **SOS button** (one tap + hold) and **voice safe-word** → instant alert with location to all guardians |
| R4 | Child can't unlock or use the phone in an emergency | SOS from the **lock-screen notification** and **power-button pattern** (Android). Voice trigger while the app runs. |
| R5 | No mobile data or balance | **SMS fallback** with a Google Maps link, sent from the child's phone |
| R6 | Phone switched off, battery died, or phone taken away | **Low-battery alert** (≤15%) and **"went offline" alert** when heartbeats stop |
| R7 | Child put in an unknown vehicle / school van speeding | **Speed alert** (configurable, e.g. > 70 km/h) and a live trail |
| R8 | Parent wants reassurance without calling | Child taps **"I'm safe" check-in** |
| R9 | Both parents (and maybe a grandparent) need alerts | **Co-guardian invite** so every guardian receives every alert |
| R10 | Stalker or abuser gets the parent's account | MFA option, app lock (biometric), login-alerts, session revoke, audit log |
| R11 | Malicious person "pairs" a child phone with their account | Pairing only via a short-lived, single-use, rate-limited code generated by the parent |

## 2. Competitor scan

| App | Strengths | Gaps we target |
|---|---|---|
| Life360 | Accurate location, crash detection, SOS, Places (2 free, more paid) | Expensive in LKR terms, no Sinhala/Tamil, no "arrive-by class" schedules |
| Google Family Link | Free, app/screen-time controls | Not built for live safety. No SOS. Weak location history. |
| Findmykids | Aimed at young kids, listen-around | Privacy concerns, subscription-heavy, no local payments |

**Positioning:** local payments (PayHere: cards, eZ Cash, mCash, Genie), LKR pricing, Sinhala/Tamil/English, and a feature built around tuition-class schedules.
In Sri Lanka, tuition-class attendance is the most common daily worry for parents.

## 3. Technical realities (honest limits)

These shape the plan. Every one of them is mitigated, not ignored.

1. **Background location**
   - **Android** needs a foreground service with a persistent notification. Google Play also requires a prominent disclosure and a declaration form.
   - **iOS** needs "Always" permission. iOS may throttle updates, so we use significant-change plus region monitoring as a fallback.
2. **Voice trigger**
   - Continuous listening when the app is fully killed is **not allowed on iOS** and is battery-heavy on Android.
   - We use **on-device** speech recognition (no audio leaves the phone). It only listens while "Guard Mode" is on, for example during the commute.
   - We say this clearly in the UI.
3. **Location accuracy**: a family locator cannot guarantee a live position. We always show "last updated X min ago" and the accuracy radius.
4. **Push reliability**: FCM high-priority messages can still be delayed by Doze mode. For SOS we also send **SMS to guardians** on paid plans.
5. **OpenStreetMap tiles** cannot be used as a production tile server under the OSM tile policy.
   - The tile URL is configurable. Production uses MapTiler, Stadia or a self-hosted server.
6. **Children's data**:
   - Location of minors is sensitive personal data under Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 (PDPA).
   - We use data minimisation, retention limits (history auto-deletes), parental consent at sign-up, and export/delete-my-data.

## 4. Payments research (Sri Lanka)

- Stripe does not onboard Sri Lankan merchants. **PayHere** is the de-facto local gateway. It supports a **Recurring API** for monthly subscriptions.
- PayHere POSTs to our `notify_url` each time a charge happens. The request includes `md5sig`, which we must verify server-side. The **Merchant Secret never leaves the server**.
- `hash = UPPER(MD5(merchant_id + order_id + amount(2dp) + currency + UPPER(MD5(merchant_secret))))`
- `md5sig = UPPER(MD5(merchant_id + order_id + payhere_amount + payhere_currency + status_code + UPPER(MD5(merchant_secret))))`
- ⚠️ These formulas follow PayHere's published integration guides. Their docs site was not reachable from the build environment, so **re-check them against the official docs before going live** (the sandbox test in `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` will also catch a mismatch).
- `status_code`: 2 = success, 0 = pending, -1 = cancelled, -2 = failed, -3 = charged back.
- The gateway layer is abstracted, so Stripe or another gateway can be added for international customers.
